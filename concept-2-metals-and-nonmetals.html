CONCEPT 2

Metals and non-metals

LEARNING OBJECTIVE
Classify elements as metals or non-metals from their physical properties, and explain why different metals suit different jobs.
BIG IDEA
Metals and non-metals split the periodic table into two families with different physical properties. Only metals are malleable and ductile — which is why we pick different metals for different jobs.

THE BIG QUESTION

Pylon cables are aluminium. House wiring is copper. Circuit boards use silver. They're all metals — so why not use the same one every time?

3 · CLASSIFY

Elements can be classified as metals or non-metals.
Metals are mainly found on the left and centre of the periodic table, while non-metals are found on the right.
A zig-zag line separates them.

Most elements are metals.

These are general patterns, not rules every element follows perfectly.

COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

  • ✕ All metals are magnetic.
  • ✕ Non-metals are always gases at room temperature.
  • ✕ Mercury being liquid means it isn't a metal.
